# Teller User Guide

Branch Operations and Frontline Transactions

Version 1.0

Prepared: 31 March 2026

Audience:

- tellers
- front-office cash staff
- branch operations support staff

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains the teller workflows used in CoreBanker and BankInsight for day-to-day frontline transaction handling.

## 2. Main Workspaces

Tellers will mainly use:

- Teller
- Transactions
- Clients
- Accounts
- Vault

## 3. Start-of-Day Checklist

1. Sign in and confirm your branch and role.
2. Check whether your till is open.
3. Review pending operational messages.
4. Confirm access to customer, account, and transaction workspaces.

## 4. Customer Verification

Before processing any value movement:

- confirm the customer identity
- confirm the correct account
- verify instrument details for cheque transactions
- verify any approval or exception message before continuing

## 5. Cash Deposits

Typical steps:

1. Open the teller workspace.
2. Search for the correct account.
3. Enter the amount.
4. Enter the transaction reference.
5. Confirm and post.

## 6. Cash Withdrawals

Typical steps:

1. Select the customer account.
2. Confirm available balance.
3. Enter amount and reference.
4. Confirm and post the withdrawal.

## 7. Cheque Deposits

The system supports:

- same-bank cheque deposit
- other-bank cheque deposit

For other-bank cheques, tellers must pay attention to:

- hold period
- clearing date
- lodged status

## 8. Cheque Withdrawals

Where cheque-book inventory control is enabled:

- select the issued cheque leaf
- do not free-type an arbitrary cheque number

Always verify:

- cheque leaf status
- account ownership
- available balance

## 9. Bulk Payment Awareness

Tellers may need to review bulk payment outcomes in the transactions workspace, even if the batch was initiated elsewhere.

Watch for:

- failed items
- partial batches
- duplicate reference concerns

## 10. Cheque Book Inventory

Operational cheque-book controls may include:

- viewing issued books
- reviewing remaining leaves
- confirming whether a leaf is available, used, or cancelled

## 11. Vault Interaction

Tellers may also interact with vault processes such as:

- till opening
- cash allocation
- cash return
- till close

## 12. Common Errors

Common teller-side issues:

- insufficient balance
- inactive account
- cheque leaf not available
- KYC or account restriction
- unauthorized action due to role

## 13. End-of-Day Responsibilities

Before sign-out:

1. confirm all required transactions are posted
2. return or reconcile cash where required
3. review till posture
4. log out securely

## 14. Best Practices

- Verify identity before every transaction.
- Use clear references.
- Never bypass cheque-book controls.
- Escalate exceptions instead of retrying blindly.
- Review transaction confirmation before moving to the next customer.
